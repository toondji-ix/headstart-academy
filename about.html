OUR STORY

Built by Educators.
Driven by Results.

Headstart Academy was born from a simple belief: every Nigerian student deserves world-class tutoring and the real chance to be the very best.

Our Mission

To provide secondary school students with the highest quality, most effective, and most engaging academic tutoring in Nigeria — empowering them to achieve scores and results they never thought possible.

We believe academic excellence isn't a gift for the few. It's a skill. It's learnable. And at Headstart, we've mastered the art of teaching it.

Our Journey

  1. 2008

    Headstart Academy founded in Ikeja, Lagos with 12 students.

  2. 2011

    First student achieves national top scorer in WAEC. The legend begins.

  3. 2015

    Expanded to 3 campuses. Over 800 students enrolled.

  4. 2018

    10-year anniversary. 10,000 alumni. First international competition medal.

  5. 2021

    Launched online hybrid programs. Nigeria-wide reach achieved.

  6. 2024

    47 national top scorers. Named Best Tutorial Centre 3rd consecutive year.
